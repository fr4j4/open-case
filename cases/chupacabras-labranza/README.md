# El Chupacabras de Labranza

Sample case of Open Case: a police mystery for 2 to 4 teams, set in a rural area near Temuco, Chile. It is written in Spanish (`languages/es/`).

- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `LICENSE-CONTENT` at the root of the repository).
- **Credit:** fr4j4 and Open Case contributors.
- **Photos:** the files in `photos/` are AI-generated images (some were edited by hand). They do not show real people or places.
- **Fiction:** all characters, companies and events are made up.
- **Spoilers:** `languages/es/guide/` and `languages/es/dossier/` contain the solution. Do not read them if you want to play.
